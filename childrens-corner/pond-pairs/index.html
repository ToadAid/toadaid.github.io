How to play together
  1. Choose one card, then another. Say the friends’ names together.
  2. If the pictures match, your pair stays face up.
  3. If they are different, look as long as you like. Choose “Turn them back” when you are ready.
  4. Keep exploring until you have all three pairs. “Start again” mixes the cards for a new game.

Use touch, a mouse, or Tab and Enter/Space on a keyboard. There is no timer—take turns at your own pace.
