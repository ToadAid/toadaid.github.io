For grown-ups: choices and a little conversation

This is an original imagined story with three moments and two welcome endings. Choices show different responses rather than awarding points. Read what happens, talk about what the friends say, and try another choice whenever you like.

Patience can choose to join in or watch beside the friends. Notice the difference between inviting someone and deciding for them. Children can share their own ideas; the personal questions have no required answer.

“Previous moment” lets you revisit your story. “Try another choice” reopens that moment and clears later choices so you can follow a different path. Starting again begins a fresh story.

This browser remembers only your story position and selected choices on this device. If saving is unavailable, you can still play for the current visit. Optional “Read to me” uses browser voices and stops whenever the story changes.

Blue Toby is the main character, Taboshi the green leaf, and Patience the red triangle. This adventure’s dialogue is new Children’s Corner storytelling, not an original Toadgod post.
