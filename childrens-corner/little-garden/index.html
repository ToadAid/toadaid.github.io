The story behind our little garden

Blue Toby and the Little Seed →
For grown-ups: play, pause, and come back

These are six pretend story days. Your child chooses when to move to the next day; there is no timer. Check the soil first: some days it is dry, and some days it is still damp. The simplified game is an imagined adventure, not a schedule for watering real plants.

This browser saves the selected flower, story day, and care progress on this device. Returning opens the same garden; it does not grow while you are away. If browser saving is unavailable, the game still works for the current visit. “Start a new garden” replaces this garden with a fresh one.

Try a real seed together with a grown-up and follow that plant’s care instructions. Ask what you can notice today, including things that are still small. Different plants need different conditions and time.

The story and dialogue are new Children’s Corner adaptations. Blue Toby is the main friend, Taboshi the green leaf, and Patience the red triangle.
