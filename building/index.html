🛡️ Why some repositories remain private

Private during hardening does not mean hidden forever

These systems contain evolving authority surfaces, inherited-code boundaries, local-machine access, personal-agent state, wallet integration, and security assumptions that must be reviewed before public release. Opening everything at once would create confusion and risk rather than trust.

The public record therefore focuses on architecture, milestones, screenshots, sanitized receipts, doctrines, and completed artifacts while the underlying forges continue to mature.

We are not hiding the mission. We are protecting the people while the machinery is still being hardened.

🌐 Path toward public source

Shared components open progressively · personal and credential state stay private
License and provenance audit

Inherited code, notices, authorship, and redistribution duties are made truthful.

Secret and history scan

Credentials, private endpoints, personal data, and sensitive history are removed or isolated.

Security and authority review

Default-off behavior, write boundaries, approvals, and receipt semantics are independently checked.

Clean installation proof

A new operator can install and verify the component without relying on private local state.

Public maintenance boundary

Documentation, support expectations, contribution rules, and release ownership are defined.

Shared contracts, schemas, doctrines, SDKs, and reference implementations may be opened individually when ready. Personal state, credentials, and operator-specific authority remain private.
